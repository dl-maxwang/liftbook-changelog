# Liftbook changelog

## 1.0.0 · First public release

November 18, 2026

- **Log a workout.** Build single lifts, complexes, or supersets. Track each rep’s weight and result, or record a timed set.
- **Track personal records.** Compare your best lifts by rep count, enter a PR manually, and calculate a training weight from your 1RM or target RPE.
- **Review your training.** Pick a day on the calendar to revisit your sets, read your notes, and compare the session with earlier bests.
